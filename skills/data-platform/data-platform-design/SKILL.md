# Data Platform Design

## Workflow

1. Identify source systems, consumers, latency needs, data volume, compliance, and ownership.
2. Define storage, compute, orchestration, quality, lineage, and access boundaries.
3. Choose batch, streaming, or hybrid patterns intentionally.
4. Document data contracts and operational artifacts.
5. Coordinate with xOps for deployment, observability, and recovery.

## Guardrails

- Do not design pipelines without quality and ownership.
- Do not ignore data freshness and lineage.
