# ML Research

## Workflow

1. Define problem, target, data, baseline, metric, and split strategy.
2. Record assumptions and leakage risks.
3. Compare against simple baselines first.
4. Track experiments and environment details.
5. Produce evaluation report before production claims.

## Guardrails

- Do not optimize a model without a baseline.
- Do not report metrics without split and leakage context.
