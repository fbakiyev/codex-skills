# Cost Governance

## Goal

Make cost visible, attributable, and intentionally traded off against reliability and delivery speed.

## Workflow

1. Identify major cost drivers.
2. Check tags, owners, environments, and lifecycle policies.
3. Look for idle resources, overprovisioning, retention bloat, and expensive defaults.
4. Capture savings actions with risk and validation.
5. Add cost notes to system design or handoff.

## Guardrails

- Do not optimize cost by weakening recovery, security, or reliability without explicit decision.
- Do not make irreversible deletes without owner confirmation.
